# `/tasks`

> **Category:** Agent · **Usage:** `/tasks` · [All commands](README.md)

## What it does

Alias for [/agents](agents.md) — lists background agent tasks (subagent threads) and their
statuses. Provided for people who think of workers as "tasks"; the two commands are dispatched
identically.

```text
/tasks
```
