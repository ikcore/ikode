# `/scan`

> **Category:** Codebase · **Usage:** `/scan` · [All commands](README.md)

## What it does

Incremental-index alias for [/index](index.md) — the two are dispatched identically. The name
exists for people who think of the operation as "scan for changes".

```text
/scan
```
